Instant translation lets you **understand**. Real life still asks you to **answer**.

A convenience-store shift, a phone call, a meeting abroad — you catch what they said, then freeze: *what do I say back?* Apps that speak for you never train your mouth. Apps that only chat with AI never put you in that moment.

**KiboTalk is a live reply assistant for real conversations.** It listens to the other person, offers a few lines you could say — meaning, target language, and reading help when you need it — and you choose one and speak it yourself.

## Why it matters

Language barriers are not only about vocabulary. They are about the seconds between hearing and responding — when confidence drops and opportunity slips away.

We believe people should keep ownership of their voice. AI can suggest; **you** still speak. That is how foreign-language life becomes practice, not spectacle — and how more people can work, study, and belong across languages without waiting until they feel “ready.”

## What you get

- **Hear them, not a bot** — built for face-to-face talk with a real counterpart.
- **A few reply choices** — typically three candidates, or a quiet skip when no prompt is needed.
- **Meaning + sayable line** — understand the intent, then say the sentence in the language of the room.
- **Stuck mid-sentence?** — get a full, speakable completion instead of a blank pause.
- **Your level, your languages** — conversation language and meaning language can differ (e.g. speak Japanese, read meaning in Chinese); difficulty follows you.
- **You open your mouth** — no TTS that talks over you; KiboTalk assists, you deliver.

## What it is not

- Not two-way simultaneous translation where each side stays in their mother tongue.
- Not an AI that speaks *as* you.
- Not “chat with a language tutor” as the main mode — the main mode is **real people, live**.
